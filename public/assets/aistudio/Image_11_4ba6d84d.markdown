## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined botanical and artisanal culinary identity. Rooted in the quiet craft of specialty coffee, heritage baking, and organic cultivation, it balances tactile warmth with high-editorial restraint. The aesthetic captures the sensory atmosphere of a glasshouse conservatory merged with an espresso atelier: steam, sunlit wood grain, dried botanicals, and warm porcelain.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tactile Heritage**: generous, breathing layouts, pristine typographic hierarchies, and organic warmth rather than synthetic starkness. Micro-interactions are gentle and grounded, reminiscent of turning heavy deckle-edged paper or lifting a ceramic saucer.

The target audience comprises discerning culinary enthusiasts, specialty coffee patrons, and design-conscious consumers seeking calm, authentic, and restorative digital experiences. Interfaces should feel cultivated, deliberate, and timeless.

## Colors

The palette reproduces organic materiality—roasted beans, sunlit honey, pressed leaves, and natural stone. The distribution balance must strictly uphold the spatial and functional hierarchy:

### Palette Structure
- **Primary Accent (Honey Gold - `#D9A441`):** Serves as the primary focal point for transactional actions, active indicators, and amber warmth.
- **Surface Dominance (Warm Cream - `#F5F0E5` & Soft Ivory - `#FAF8F0`):** Occupies **55%** of the UI canvas. Soft Ivory serves as elevated panels and card faces, while Warm Cream grounds page viewports.
- **Botanical Cadence (Deep Forest Green - `#263F2C`, Moss Green - `#526447`, Sage Green - `#78866A`):** Occupies **20%** of the system. Used for immersive full-bleed footers, seasonal badges, terroir tags, and structural grounding dividers.
- **Earth & Typography (Espresso Brown - `#38291F` & Cocoa Brown - `#594132`):** Occupies **15%** of the visual weight. High-contrast typography, deep iconography, and tactile dark frames replace harsh digital blacks.
- **Warm Accents & Amber Embers (Honey Gold - `#D9A441` & Burnt Amber - `#B96D35`):** Reserved for **10%** of the interface. High-intent CTAs, key status callouts, and hover transitions.

### Auxiliary & Secondary Values
- **Beeswax Beige (`#E5D3A9`):** Accent surface fill, warm container borders, and soft interactive selection fills.
- **Sand Beige (`#D8C8AE`):** Structural borders, hair-line grid rules, and card outlines.
- **Muted Olive (`#A0A17B`):** Specific origin tags, botanical tasting notes, and gentle nature markers.
- **Dark Roast (`#241A15`):** Maximum contrast dark canvas areas, nighttime modes, or deepest typographic weights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary charm of `Playfair Display` with the crisp legibility of `Plus Jakarta Sans`. 

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Use `Playfair Display` in italic variants for secondary headline accents, origin stories, roast profiles, or botanical adjectives (e.g., "*Single Origin* Yirgacheffe", "*Slow-Fermented* Sourdough").
- **Structural Body Copy:** Set body text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` utilizing `#38291F` (Espresso Brown) for primary reading lines and `#594132` (Cocoa Brown) for metadata, captions, and secondary paragraphs.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** `label-sm` is rendered in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic pressed copper plate or letterpress menu stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial grid with open margins that prioritize airiness and visual calm over density.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout. Gutter: `2rem` (`32px`), Margin: `3rem` (`48px`). Content container max-width: `1320px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`), Margin: `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`), Margin: `1rem` (`16px`).

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere strictly to multiples of `space-md` (`16px`). Major narrative breaks utilize `space-xl` (`40px`) or paired multiples (`80px`), allowing botanical imagery and artisanal menu items to stand unencumbered.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal layering and warm, diffuse ambient shadows**, avoiding sterile grey digital drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Shadow Layers
1. **Flat / Flush:** 1px hairline border using `#D8C8AE` (Sand Beige) or `#E5D3A9` (Beeswax Beige). No shadow.
2. **Elevated (Resting Cards):** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(56, 41, 31, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(56, 41, 31, 0.04)`. A gentle brown-tinted ambient warmth mimicking morning raking light.
3. **Floating (Flyouts & Dropdowns):** `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(56, 41, 31, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(56, 41, 31, 0.06)`.
4. **Modal / Overlay:** Backed with `rgba(36, 26, 21, 0.6)` (Dark Roast with 60% opacity) coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

Avoid high-contrast artificial glow; Honey Gold (`#D9A441`) illuminates interactive hover accents with a subtle amber back-tint: `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The interface specifies a uniform corner radius of **12px** (`rounded-xl` in conventional scales, mapping to level `2`), giving forms the organic, tactile softness of tumbled stone, sculpted wood, and curved ceramic dishware.

- **Cards, Modals, Panels:** 12px (`0.75rem` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, Segmented Controls:** 12px (`0.75rem` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Small Badges & Pills:** Fully circular / pill (`9999px`) to visually differentiate metadata from actionable containers.
- **Botanical Image Masks:** 12px rounded edges with optional organic arch variations on hero containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#D9A441` (Honey Gold), label `#241A15` (Dark Roast), font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold, height `48px`, border radius `12px`. Hover shifts background to `#B96D35` (Burnt Amber) with `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px 0 rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `#263F2C` (Deep Forest Green), label `#FAF8F0` (Soft Ivory), border radius `12px`. Hover transforms background to `#526447` (Moss Green).
- **Tertiary / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1px solid border in `#38291F`, label `#38291F`. Hover adds soft tint of `#E5D3A9` (Beeswax Beige).

### Cards & Menu Containers
- **Base Menu Card:** Background `#FAF8F0` (Soft Ivory), 1px solid border in `#D8C8AE` (Sand Beige), border radius `12px`. Padding `space-lg` (`24px`).
- **Feature Card (Botanical Highlight):** Background `#F5F0E5`, accent header strip or background highlight `#263F2C` with Soft Ivory text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tasting Note / Origin Chip:** Background `#E5D3A9` (Beeswax Beige) at 40% opacity, text `#263F2C` (Deep Forest Green), border `1px solid #78866A`, border-radius `9999px`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Roast Level Badge:** Background `#38291F` (Espresso Brown), text `#F5F0E5` (Warm Cream), uppercase `label-sm`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FAF8F0` (Soft Ivory), text `#38291F`, placeholder `#594132` (50% opacity), border `1.5px solid #D8C8AE`. Focused state features border `#D9A441` and subtle glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 164, 65, 0.2)`. Height `48px`, border radius `12px`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked: 1.5px border `#78866A` against `#FAF8F0`. Checked: Fill `#263F2C` with Soft Ivory checkmark icon or Honey Gold `#D9A441` center disc.

### Lists & Dividers
- **Artisanal Menu List Item:** Separated by subtle horizontal lines (`1px solid #D8C8AE`). Dish name in `Playfair Display` 18px (`#38291F`), followed by leader dots or open whitespace, and price in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (`#B96D35`). Ingredients set in `body-sm` (`#594132`).
